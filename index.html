Security Awareness Exercise

You Scanned an Educational QR Code

This demonstration highlights how easily QR codes can redirect users to arbitrary external destinations.

1. The Threat

QR codes obscure the destination URL from plain sight. Attackers place them on parking meters, physical posters, or phishing emails to bypass standard email inspection filters.

2. Mobile Blindspots

Scanning codes on personal phones often bypasses corporate endpoint monitoring and security agents, landing directly on mobile browsers with limited domain visibility.

3. Credential Exposure

A malicious link can present a realistic login form designed to harvest corporate credentials or session tokens without raising immediate alarms.

4. Device Takeover

Unvetted endpoints may attempt to push malicious profiles, payload downloaders, or force device enrollment under unauthorized mobile management systems.

Key Defensive Steps

  • Always inspect the resolved URL in your camera application before tapping to open.
  • Never input corporate credentials on sites accessed directly via an unexpected QR code.
  • Use dedicated multi-factor authentication methods like FIDO2/passkeys that bind directly to verified origins.
